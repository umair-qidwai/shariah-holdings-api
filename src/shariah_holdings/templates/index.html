What this data means

Holdings come from official fund-publisher sources and are normalized into an exact ticker union. A ticker's presence reflects reported ETF membership, not individualized religious or investment advice.

Refreshes are fail-closed: incomplete, stale, or malformed source data is rejected, so a failed refresh cannot replace the last validated generation.
